01 / about

Support that scales with the team.

At the MSP I started on the phones and finished as the bridge between the C-suite and the techs on the ground. Along the way I mentored every new hire as the help desk grew from two people to a crew of six.

My favorite work is the unglamorous kind: finding the chronic blockers nobody owns — missing MDM portal access, unknown router credentials, unmonitored UPS alerts, undocumented platforms — and turning each one into a documented, permanent fix in the team knowledge base.

Before IT, I spent years in restaurants, including two as a shift supervisor running floor and kitchen crews of 9–15. It's where I learned to stay calm when everything is on fire at once.
